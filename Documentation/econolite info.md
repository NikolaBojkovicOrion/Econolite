# Econolite Information

## Company Overview

Econolite is an intelligent transportation systems company. Its customers include cities, public agencies, and transportation departments, primarily across North America.

The company focuses on improving roadway mobility, safety, and efficiency through a combination of:

- Traffic-management software
- Traffic signal controllers
- Traffic cabinets and roadway equipment
- Sensors and vehicle detection
- Cloud platforms and AI
- Transportation engineering
- Systems integration and customer services

Econolite is therefore a **product company**, but it is not only a software company. Its products combine software, hardware, cloud services, data, and professional implementation work.

## Business Domain

Econolite operates in the intelligent transportation and smart-mobility domain. Its systems help transportation agencies:

- Coordinate traffic signals
- Detect congestion and incidents
- Monitor roadway conditions
- Manage intersections and traffic networks
- Analyze historical and real-time transportation data
- Improve safety and reduce delays
- Support connected and automated vehicles
- Modernize legacy transportation infrastructure

This domain is different from a typical CRUD application because software can interact with physical roadway equipment and influence real-world traffic operations. Reliability, auditability, security, backward compatibility, and predictable behavior are important.

## Software Products

Publicly identified products include:

- **Centracs ATMS**: advanced traffic management and signal operations
- **EOS Controller Software**: traffic signal controller software
- **PTV Optima**: AI-powered, real-time traffic management
- **PTV Flows**: cloud-based traffic monitoring and traffic prediction
- **PTV Visum**: transport planning and network modeling
- **PTV Vissim**: traffic and multimodal simulation
- **PTV Vistro**: traffic engineering and signal timing analysis
- **PTV Hub**: cloud collaboration and computation platform
- **PTV Model2Go**: cloud-generated transport models

The wider mobility portfolio also covers pedestrian simulation, public-transport planning, digital twins, analytics, prediction, and AI.

## Hardware and Infrastructure Products

Econolite also provides:

- Traffic signal controllers
- Traffic cabinets
- Radar detection
- Video detection
- Traffic sensors
- Connected-vehicle infrastructure
- On-site traffic-management equipment

## Public Scale Metrics

An exact number of internal software projects is not publicly available. Public material instead identifies deployment and company-scale metrics, including approximately:

- 515 systems deployed
- 160,000+ cabinets
- 120,000+ traffic controllers
- 160,000+ sensors
- 2,900 cities served
- 130+ countries
- 1,500 employees across the broader Econolite and PTV/Umovity group

These are deployed systems and customers, not the number of repositories or development projects.
